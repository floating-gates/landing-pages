## Exit Demo mode and set up your Hub

If no actions are taken inside the platform, the only hub available to you is the demo at [demo.floating-gates.com](https://demo.floating-gates.com). From the demo you can see all the features that are available to consumers. However, to make your CAD available you need to follow the steps listed below:

![Exiting demo mode](../data/images/docs-images/publish-your-cad-online-1.png)

## Choose your Hub address

![Selecting your URL](../data/images/docs-images/publish-your-cad-online-2.png)

Once logged in you will reach the dashboard, which asks you what kind of subscription plan you want and at what address you plan to host your client App:

![Choosing a hub address](../data/images/docs-images/quick-start-2.png)

Select the name of the hub and the plan you want based on the features you would like to use. The payment is held by GoCardless:

![GoCardless](../data/images/docs-images/quick-start-3.png)

## How direct debit is set up

Once you choose your selected plan you will be redirected to a page similar to the one below, managed by GoCardless:

![GoCardless mandate](../data/images/docs-images/publish-your-cad-online-3.png)

Then you are asked to input the IBAN and account holder name.

![Entering bank details](../data/images/docs-images/publish-your-cad-online-4.png)

Once the procedure is completed your CAD will be ready for the world to access.
